About

01 / Executive Summary

Mechanical Engineering junior at CSU.

I'm focused on aerospace propulsion and vehicle systems. My tools span SolidWorks and ANSYS on the design side, ESP32 firmware and PID controls on the electronics side, and MATLAB/Simulink for simulation. I also do hands-on fabrication — machining, 3D printing, composites.

I keep good documentation, because it forces clarity on decisions that are easy to make sloppily and hard to revisit later.

02 / Core Competencies

What I work on.

Systems Integration & Engineering Design

Mechanical, electronic, and control subsystems don't get designed in isolation. I work across those boundaries — CAD assemblies, interface specs, firmware — and make sure the connections between subsystems are defined before they become problems.

Technical Leadership & Project Management

I write the specs, run design reviews, and track what's blocking progress. The goal is to keep the team doing engineering work rather than figuring out who owns what.

Hands-On Prototyping & Manufacturing

FDM printing, machined aluminum, composite layups, sheet metal — I pick based on what the part needs, not what's easiest. I also build the test rigs, not just the parts being tested.

03 / Philosophy

How I work.

I want the work to show what I built, why I made the calls I did, what the data said, and what I'd do differently. The hardware is the output. The decisions behind it are the actual work.
